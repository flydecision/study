📊 Weather model accuracy comparison against real station data

Continuous 24/7 automated analysis comparing weather model forecasts with real wind measurements from weather stations located at paragliding takeoffs. The ultimate goal is to discover which model performs best: overall, or specifically by parameters, locations, times, or forecast horizons.
  • 3 Models: AromeHD 1.3km / ICON-EU 7km / ECMWF IFS 9km
  • 97 Weather Stations: Located at 97 paragliding takeoffs
  • 3 Parameters: Average wind speed, maximum gust and direction
  • 6 Time Slots: Real data and forecast snapshots at 9, 11, 13, 15, 17, and 19h daily (local time CET/CEST Europe/Madrid)
  • 7 Forecast Horizons: +6h / +1 day / +2 days / +3 days / +4 days / +5 days / +6 days
Open source data (CSV) | Open source code (GitHub). Everything is updated automatically, so you can view or download the data to verify it, report errors, suggest improvements, or perform your own analysis.

ℹ️ How is the Champion Score calculated:

The score represents the model's average ranking position across all parameters (wind, gust, direction) and forecast horizons (+6h, +1 day, +2 days, +3 days).

For every valid comparison, models are ranked by their absolute error (MAE):

  • 1st place (best) = 1 point
  • 2nd place = 2 points
  • 3rd place (worst) = 3 points

A lower score is better (a perfect score of 1.00 means the model won every single comparison). The number in parentheses (matches) indicates how many valid comparisons were made to calculate this average.

This overall ranking already takes into account that AromeHD does not compete in the +2 and +3 days horizons, and ICON-EU does not go beyond +3 days. From +4 to +6 days only ECMWF is evaluated (no model comparison, just how its accuracy decays), so those horizons are excluded from the overall ranking.

ℹ️ Quick Glossary:

• MAE (Mean Absolute Error): The "magnitude of the error", regardless of whether it is an overestimation or underestimation.

• MBE (Mean Bias Error): Indicates whether the model tends to predict more or less wind than actual (e.g., -4 km/h means it tends to underestimate, so actual wind will likely be higher).

• RMSE (Root Mean Square Error): Similar to MAE, but penalizes large errors more heavily. When RMSE is noticeably higher than MAE, it means the model is usually reasonably accurate but occasionally makes large mistakes — rather than being consistently off by a similar amount every time.

• n: Number of valid forecast-vs-reality comparisons accumulated so far for that specific parameter, model, and forecast horizon. Since the study runs continuously, this number keeps growing over time, so results become statistically more reliable the longer the study runs. The system and automatic analysis generate approximately 2000 new samples per day (this rate assumes normal system uptime, station and system operation and only valid matched samples are counted).

For all statistical calculations on the Direction parameter, a minimum speed threshold of 8 km/h has been established (slightly higher than the ICAO/METAR standard of 3 knots / 5.5 km/h). The goal is to minimize what is called in meteorological statistics "the noise of calms" (large random direction fluctuations in light winds).

ℹ️ Global Score (0-10) is calculated weighted: Penalty = (Avg MAE × 0.4) + (Gust MAE × 0.3) + (Dir Error × 0.08 × 0.3). Score = max(0, 10 - Penalty / 1.5).

ℹ️ AromeHD does not compete in the +2 and +3 days horizons, and ICON-EU does not go beyond +3 days. From +4 to +6 days only ECMWF is evaluated (no model comparison, just how its accuracy decays), so those horizons are excluded from the overall ranking.

ℹ️ How is the Overall Winner calculated?
A score from 0 to 10 is assigned to each model at each takeoff (10 would be a perfect forecast). The score is calculated by deducting points based on error across the 3 parameters:
  • 40% of score: Average wind speed error.
  • 30% of score: Maximum gust error.
  • 30% of score: Wind direction error.
