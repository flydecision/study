📊 Weather model accuracy comparison against real station data

Continuous 24/7 automated analysis comparing weather model forecasts with real wind measurements from weather stations located at paragliding takeoffs. The ultimate goal is to discover which model performs best: overall, or specifically by parameters, locations, times, or forecast horizons.
  • 3 Models: AromeHD 1.3km / ICON-EU 7km / ECMWF IFS 9km
  • 72 Weather Stations: Located at 72 paragliding takeoffs
  • 3 Parameters: Average wind speed, maximum gust, and direction
  • 6 Time Slots: Real data and forecast snapshots at 9, 11, 13, 15, 17, and 19h daily (local time CET/CEST Europe/Madrid)
  • 4 Forecast Horizons: +6h / +24h / +48h / +72h

ℹ️ How is the Champion Score calculated:

The score represents the model's average ranking position across all parameters (wind, gust, direction) and forecast horizons (+6h, +24h, +48h, +72h).

For every valid comparison, models are ranked by their absolute error (MAE):

  • 1st place (best) = 1 point
  • 2nd place = 2 points
  • 3rd place (worst) = 3 points

A lower score is better (a perfect score of 1.00 means the model won every single comparison). The number in parentheses (matches) indicates how many valid comparisons were made to calculate this average. ECMWF is fairly excluded from gust comparisons.

This overall ranking already takes into account that AromeHD does not compete in +48h and +72h horizons.

🏆 Parameter Ranking for +24h Forecast Horizon:

Averages the absolute error (MAE) for average wind and gust, as well as accumulated bias at 24 hours out (ECMWF does not compete in gust as it does not record that parameter). For direction, it averages the absolute angular error (0° to 180°) and displays in parentheses the signed directional bias (+ / - depending on clockwise or counter-clockwise rotation relative to real wind).

ℹ️ Quick Glossary:

• Mean Absolute Error (MAE): The "magnitude of the error", regardless of whether it is an overestimation or underestimation.

• Bias: Indicates whether the model tends to predict more or less wind than actual (e.g., -4 km/h means it tends to underestimate, so actual wind will likely be higher).

For all statistical calculations on the Direction parameter, a minimum speed threshold of 8 km/h has been established (slightly higher than the ICAO/METAR standard of 3 knots / 5.5 km/h). The goal is to minimize what is called in meteorological statistics "the noise of calms" (large random direction fluctuations in light winds).

AromeHD does not compete in +48h and +72h horizons.

ℹ️ How is the Overall Winner calculated?
A score from 0 to 10 is assigned to each model at each takeoff (10 would be a perfect forecast). The score is calculated by deducting points based on error across the 3 parameters:
  • 40% of score: Average wind speed error.
  • 30% of score: Maximum gust error.
  • 30% of score: Wind direction error.

Since maximum gust data is not collected for ECMWF, its score is reweighted to 55% average wind and 45% direction so it is not penalized for lacking gust data.
